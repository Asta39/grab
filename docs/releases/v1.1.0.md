Grab 1.1.0 is the fun update: Grab Club, a buddy who cheers you on, and new ways to use what you save.

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |

## What's new

**Grab Club**
- Streaks with a weekly freeze, XP, five levels and 24 badges, all in Trophies
- A celebration and a sound when you unlock something, and confetti at 10, 50, 100 and more files
- Playful mode switch in Settings turns all of it off

**The Grab buddy**
- A yellow blob on the dock who cheers when a download saves, naps at night and reacts to what you grab: headphones for a song, a nightcap after 11 pm, a crown at a milestone
- Eight hats to earn, each drawn in every mood

**Make it yours**
- Hats, six celebrations (hearts, music notes, popcorn, gold, buddy rain), nine sound packs, eight docks (some animated) and audio cover styles, unlocked by levels and badges
- Try on anything locked before you earn it

**New tools**
- Grab Pal: describe what you're looking for and it finds it in your library, fuzzy and typo-proof. Add your own Groq key in Settings for smarter search
- Make a GIF or a WhatsApp sticker from any saved video (Android)
- Grab Wrapped: your year as a story, with a poster to share
- A weekly recap card on Home from Saturday to Monday
- Home screen widget with your streak and a one-tap grab (Android)
- Shake your phone to grab the copied link (Android)

## Fixes

- Opening a saved song, or a video whose picture the phone can't decode, no longer closes Grab on Android
