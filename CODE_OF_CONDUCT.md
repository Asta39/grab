# Code of Conduct

Grab is a small, friendly open-source project. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives — is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Grab to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique the code, never the person.
- Stay on topic and keep discussions useful to others who find them later.
- Respect people's time: search before opening an issue, and include what's needed to help.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption.
- Using Grab's spaces to share or request pirated material, or to plan breaking a site's terms.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all of Grab's project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you see or experience something that breaks this code, tell the maintainer privately through the contact details on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Asta39). If you can't reach them that way, open an issue titled **"Conduct report — please contact me"** with no details, and the maintainer will get in touch privately.

All reports are taken seriously, handled quickly, and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Responses are proportionate: usually a private warning first, and a ban only for serious or repeated harm.
